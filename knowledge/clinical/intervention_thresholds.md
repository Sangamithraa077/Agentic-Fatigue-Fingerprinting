# Clinical and Ergonomic Intervention Thresholds for Fatigue Management

Evidence-based intervention thresholds for occupational and rehabilitation fatigue management:

**RPE-Based Thresholds:**
* **RPE < 11:** Light work zone — no intervention needed, continuous monitoring.
* **RPE 12–14:** Moderate work zone — monitor closely, consider micro-rest (<30s) if sustained.
* **RPE 15–17 (Threshold):** Heavy exertion zone — reduce load by 20–30% or insert a structured 60–90 second rest window. The threshold implemented in this framework is **RPE ≥ 15.0**.
* **RPE ≥ 18:** Critical zone — immediate task termination, minimum 3-minute active recovery.

**EMG-Based Thresholds:**
* MDF decline **> 10%** from resting baseline: Early fatigue marker — begin monitoring intensity.
* MDF decline **> 25%**: Clinically significant fatigue — intervention recommended.
* MDF decline **> 40%**: Approaching volitional exhaustion — immediate load reduction.

**Combined Criterion:**
When both RPE ≥ 15 AND MDF decline > 20% are simultaneously present, intervention is strongly recommended regardless of subjective perception.
