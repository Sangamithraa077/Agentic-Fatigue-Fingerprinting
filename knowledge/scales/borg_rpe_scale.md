# Borg Rating of Perceived Exertion (RPE 6-20) Scale

The Borg RPE scale measures subjective perceived physical exertion, designed to linearly approximate adult heart rate (RPE x 10 ≈ HR in beats per minute):

* **6:** No exertion at all (Complete rest, baseline HR ~60 BPM).
* **7–10:** Extremely light / very light exertion (Minimal metabolic load).
* **11–12:** Light exertion (Comfortable working pace, aerobic steady state).
* **13–14:** Somewhat hard (Early fatigue onset, onset of blood lactate accumulation).
* **15–16:** Hard / Heavy exertion (Significant fatigue, warning threshold for clinical/ergonomic intervention).
* **17–18:** Very hard exertion (Severe exhaustion, acute injury risk, micro-tremor arm jitter).
* **19–20:** Extremely hard / Maximal exertion (Volitional exhaustion, complete muscle failure).

**Cautionary Distinction:** Perceived exertion (RPE) integrates central effort perception, dyspnea, and muscle ache. It is not identical to localized physiological muscle fatigue (EMG spectral shift).
