# Research System Safety Boundaries and Medical Disclaimer

**This system is a research prototype for physiological fatigue research and ergonomic evaluation.**

**What this system is:**
* A multimodal fatigue monitoring and assessment tool built for research, sports science, and occupational ergonomics evaluation.
* A decision support system that flags physiological indicators for human review.
* A software prototype validated on the WSD4FEDSRM public benchmark dataset.

**What this system is NOT:**
* A medical device, diagnostic tool, or clinical instrument.
* A substitute for professional medical advice, clinical evaluation, or rehabilitation treatment.
* A validated real-time clinical monitoring system (hardware integration is a research prototype stage).

**Limitations to state explicitly:**
* Predictions are based on a single public dataset (34 participants). Accuracy for any new individual or population may differ.
* The Borg RPE target is a subjective perceived exertion measure, not a direct measure of physiological muscle fatigue biomarkers (such as blood lactate, VO2, or direct nerve conduction velocity).
* In any medical, clinical, or rehabilitation context, consult a qualified physiotherapist or sports medicine physician.
