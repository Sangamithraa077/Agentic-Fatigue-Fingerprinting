# Cardiorespiratory Strain and Autonomic Tone from Photoplethysmography

Photoplethysmography (PPG) captures peripheral pulsatile blood volume changes, providing vital cardiorespiratory metrics complementary to neuromuscular EMG:

1. **Heart Rate (HR) Elevation:** Progressively rises during physical exercise due to sympathetic activation and parasympathetic (vagal) withdrawal.
2. **Heart Rate Variability (HRV) Depression:** Parasympathetic markers such as RMSSD (root mean square of successive differences) and pNN50 decrease sharply as exertion escalates.
3. **Cardiovascular Drift:** Under continuous workloads, HR continues to drift upward even when power output is constant, reflecting cumulative physiological exertion and thermoregulatory load.
