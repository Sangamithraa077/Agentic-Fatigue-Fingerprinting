# IMU Tempo Analysis and Inter-Repetition Cycle Variability During Fatigue

Repetitive shoulder rotation tasks (as in WSD4FEDSRM) exhibit characteristic temporal deterioration in IMU signals as fatigue progresses:

**Repetition Tempo Slowing:**
The duration of each rotation cycle (internal-to-external-return) lengthens as the prime movers fatigue. This is detectable from gyroscope angular velocity zero-crossings — the time between velocity sign changes increases progressively.

**Cycle-to-Cycle Kinematic Variability:**
Coefficient of variation (CV) of cycle duration increases with fatigue. A CV below 5% indicates a well-controlled motion pattern; a CV exceeding 15–20% indicates neuromuscular coordination breakdown. This metric is derived from the pelvis and sternum IMU placements in WSD4FEDSRM (the most stable reference segments).

**Arm Speed Reduction:**
The peak angular velocity magnitude recorded at the upper arm and forearm IMUs decreases under fatigue as neuromuscular power output declines, even when the external load remains constant.
