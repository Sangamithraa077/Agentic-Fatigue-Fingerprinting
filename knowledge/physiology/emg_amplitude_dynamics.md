# EMG Amplitude (RMS/MAV) Dynamics and Motor Unit Recruitment

Under submaximal isometric or repetitive dynamic contractions, the Root Mean Square (RMS) and Mean Absolute Value (MAV) of surface EMG exhibit a characteristic rise as fatigue develops.

To maintain task demand and target force output as individual muscle fibers fatigue:
1. The central nervous system recruits additional, higher-threshold motor units according to Henneman's size principle.
2. The firing rates of active motor units discharge more synchronously.
3. The spatial summation of action potentials produces higher amplitude electrical waveforms recorded at the skin surface.

The combination of rising RMS amplitude coupled with falling Median Frequency (MDF) provides the classic bivariate physiological fingerprint of localized muscular fatigue.
