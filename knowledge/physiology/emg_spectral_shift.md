# Surface EMG Spectral Compression and Conduction Velocity

During sustained or repetitive muscular contractions, localized muscle fatigue manifests as a shift in the surface EMG power spectrum toward lower frequencies. 

Both the Mean Power Frequency (MPF) and Median Frequency (MDF) decrease progressively as fatigue develops. This physiological phenomenon is primarily driven by:
1. Slowing of muscle fiber action potential conduction velocity (MFCV) due to extracellular accumulation of metabolic byproducts (such as lactic acid and hydrogen ions H+ lowering intramuscular pH).
2. Synchronization of motor unit firing as central motor drive increases to compensate for declining contractile force.

A drop in MDF greater than 10-15% from resting baseline is a recognized physiological indicator of localized neuromuscular fatigue.
