# Motor Unit Recruitment, De-recruitment, and Myoelectric Fatigue Index

During sustained submaximal voluntary contractions at 30–60% MVIC (the load regime in WSD4FEDSRM), muscle force production is maintained by progressive motor unit recruitment and rate coding.

**Recruitment Pattern:**
1. Small, slow-twitch (type I) motor units activate first (Henneman size principle).
2. As fatigue progresses and type I units fail, the CNS recruits larger, fast-twitch (type II) units — which are metabolically costly and fatigue quickly.
3. Type II recruitment is visible on surface EMG as both spectral compression (lower MDF) and amplitude increase (higher RMS).

**Myoelectric Fatigue Index (MFI):**
A slope-based measure of fatigue tracking the rate of MDF decrease over the exercise trial:
$$\text{MFI} = \frac{\Delta \text{MDF}}{\Delta t} $$
A steeper negative slope (e.g., MDF falling faster than 2 Hz per second) indicates severe neuromuscular fatigue.
