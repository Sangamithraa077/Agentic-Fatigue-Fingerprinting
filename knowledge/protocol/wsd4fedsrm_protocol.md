# WSD4FEDSRM Experimental Protocol & Sensor Configuration

The WSD4FEDSRM dataset consists of 34 healthy participants (P01–P34) executing controlled shoulder internal and external rotation tasks to volitional exhaustion across 3 intensity bands: 30–40%, 40–50%, and 50–60% of Maximum Voluntary Isometric Contraction (MVIC).

**Sensors & Modalities Recorded:**
1. **Surface EMG (1000 Hz, 6 Muscles):** Anterior Deltoid, Posterior Deltoid, Infraspinatus, Upper Trapezius, Latissimus Dorsi, Pectoralis Major.
2. **IMU (100 Hz, 6 Body Placements):** Hand, Forearm, Upper Arm, Shoulder, Sternum, Pelvis (each providing 3-axis acceleration, gyroscope, and magnetometer).
3. **Finger PPG (200 Hz):** Optical pulse photoplethysmography waveform.
4. **Reference Target:** Self-reported Borg RPE (6–20) recorded at periodic intervals and exhaustion.
