# Shoulder Rotation Biomechanics and Muscle Roles During WSD4FEDSRM Tasks

The WSD4FEDSRM protocol uses **shoulder internal rotation (IR)** and **external rotation (ER)** against a spring-based resistance load set to 30–60% MVIC.

**Primary muscles for Internal Rotation:**
* **Anterior Deltoid** — primary IR agonist; fatigues earliest in IR trials.
* **Pectoralis Major (clavicular head)** — assists IR, especially at lower resistance intensities.
* **Latissimus Dorsi** — secondary IR muscle; typically fatigues later in heavier MVIC bands.
* **Subscapularis** (not measured by surface EMG in WSD4FEDSRM due to depth).

**Primary muscles for External Rotation:**
* **Infraspinatus** — primary ER agonist; most sensitive to fatigue in ER trials (largest MDF decline).
* **Posterior Deltoid** — assists ER, especially in the end range of motion.
* **Upper Trapezius** — stabilizes the scapula during ER; its fatigue signature differs from prime movers.

**Diagnostic rule:**
If SHAP attributions show Infraspinatus as the top driver during an external rotation trial, this is physiologically consistent. If Anterior Deltoid leads during an ER trial, it suggests compensatory activation — a sign of coordination breakdown under fatigue.
